Poster presentation in Nanoday! 
Nanoday is a yearly forum for faculty, postdocs, and students engaged in nanoscience and nanotechnology research <a href="https://www.vanderbilt.edu/vinse/2023/09/29/23rd-annual-nanoscience-nanotechnology-forum-nanoday-12823/">2023 Nanoday</a> . I presented a poster titled "Porous silicon on paper for rapid diagnostic tests" advising under <a href="https://www.vanderbilt.edu/vinse/personnel/?bio=sharon%20weiss">Dr. Sharon Weiss </a>. 
It was amazing to share my research as well as learn other research fields!
