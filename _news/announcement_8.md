I received the Parkhutik Prize at the 14th International Conference on Porous Semiconductors – Science and Technology (PSST) for my research on fluid-flow control in paper-based porous silicon biosensors. The work focuses on understanding how microfluidic transport influences assay performance and optical sensing in integrated diagnostic platforms.
