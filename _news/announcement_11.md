I gave an oral presentation of our work on integrated porous silicon biosensing technologies at SPIE Photonics West 2025, with the research published in the Frontiers in Biological Detection: From Nanosensors to Systems XVII proceedings. The project explores the integration of optical sensing, microfluidics, and assay engineering toward quantitative point-of-care diagnostic platforms.
