I am pleased to share my first-author work on a porous silicon-on-paper platform for quantitative rapid diagnostic testing was published in ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces. We integrated porous silicon optical sensors with paper-based fluid transport to improve biomolecular delivery and sensing performance. The study demonstrates how device architecture and fluid-flow engineering can be used to enhance the performance of low-cost optical biosensors.
