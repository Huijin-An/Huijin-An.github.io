Publications

  • 2026
    Regulating the Nano–Bio Interface| Converging Electrochemical and Photonic Biosensing for Wearable Diagnostics
    Nano Letters

    This mini-review highlights the convergence of electrochemical and photonic biosensing as a systems-level strategy for wearable diagnostics, where complementary electrical and optical signals probe the same dynamic nano–bio interface.

  • 2025
    Porous silicon on paper| A platform for quantitative rapid diagnostic tests
    ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces

    The first integration of open-ended porous silicon membranes with paper substrates enables pump-free flow-through biosensing, improving molecular transport and producing concentration-dependent optical responses for protein detection.

  • 2025
    Porous silicon-on-paper sensors| factors affecting biosensing performance
    SPIE Frontiers in Biological Detection

    A paper-based porous silicon biosensor improves molecular transport and enables enhanced quantitative protein detection compared with conventional PSi films on silicon. By tuning wicking and absorbent paper layers, the platform can control incubation and testing time, supporting low-cost, sensitive rapid diagnostics.

  • 2021
    Vacuum-filtration fabrication for diverse conductive transparent cellulose electronic devices
    Cellulose

    Develop a simple filtration and solvent-evaporation method to fabricate transparent conductive nitrocellulose electrodes, using carbon nanomaterials or silver nanowires, for touch sensors and paper-based electronic devices.
